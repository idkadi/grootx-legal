# GrootX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

## Introduction

GrootX is a fan-made Discord bot and collectible card game inspired by Marvel characters and media. GrootX is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with Marvel Entertainment, The Walt Disney Company, or any related entities.

## Information We Collect

GrootX stores only the information necessary for gameplay and bot functionality, including:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Server IDs
* Card collections
* Deck configurations
* In-game currencies and balances
* Cooldowns and timers
* Battle and progression data
* User settings related to the bot

GrootX does not collect:

* Passwords
* Payment information
* Personal addresses
* Private Discord messages

## How We Use Data

Stored data is used solely to:

* Provide game functionality
* Save collections and progress
* Manage battles and rewards
* Prevent abuse and exploitation
* Improve the bot experience

## Data Sharing

GrootX does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

## Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide bot services. Users may request deletion of their stored game data by contacting the bot owner.

## Third-Party Services

GrootX operates through Discord and may use hosting providers and databases to function. Users are also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## Fan Project Disclaimer

GrootX is a non-commercial fan project. All Marvel-related names, characters, images, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
